About NEONIX

NEONIX is a futuristic, lightweight, browser-based search engine interface built with pure HTML5, CSS3, and Vanilla JavaScript.

Privacy & Local Architecture: Your search history and preference settings are stored 100% locally on your browser using HTML5 localStorage. No search tracking or server analytics are recorded by NEONIX.

Keyboard Shortcuts:
• Ctrl + K or / — Focus search input
• Esc — Close modals and dropdowns
